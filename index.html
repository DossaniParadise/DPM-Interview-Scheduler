Approved candidates

Only people on this list can book, and each can book once. Paste one per line as First Last, phone — commas or tabs both work. Resumes are attached after adding — they upload to Filestack, same as PayMi.

Create interview slots

Pick a posting, a date or date range, and a window. It fills the window with back-to-back slots — 10am to 2pm at 30 minutes makes 8. Existing slots are left alone, so re-running is safe.

Assigned staff see these interviews under their own "Interviews" tab.
